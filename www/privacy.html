🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 8, 2026

Summary: Math Quest Portal does not collect, store, or share any personal data. All information stays on your device.

📱 About This App

Math Quest Portal is an educational math practice application designed for students in grades 1-8. The app provides practice exams with competition-style questions to help students improve their math skills.

🚫 Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect any personal information. Specifically, we do NOT collect:

  • Names or personal identifiers
  • Email addresses
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers
  • Usage analytics
  • Cookies or tracking data

💾 Local Data Storage

The app stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • User profiles: Display names and usernames you create
  • Exam history: Your practice exam scores and progress
  • Preferences: App settings you configure

This data is stored using your browser's or device's local storage and never leaves your device. You can delete this data at any time by clearing your browser data or uninstalling the app.

🌐 Network Access

Math Quest Portal works entirely offline. The app does not:

  • Make network requests to external servers
  • Send any data over the internet
  • Require an internet connection to function

👶 Children's Privacy

This app is designed to be safe for children of all ages. Since we do not collect any data, there is no risk of children's personal information being collected or shared. We comply with:

  • Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)
  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)

🔄 Third-Party Services

Math Quest Portal does not use any third-party services, SDKs, or analytics tools. The app is completely self-contained.

📝 Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this privacy policy, we will post the new version here with an updated date. Since the app does not collect data, significant changes are unlikely.
