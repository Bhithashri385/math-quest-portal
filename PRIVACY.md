# Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-02-09

Think Math AI is a kid-friendly educational app that helps children practice math for competitive exams through interactive quests and challenges.

## No data collected

- We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.
- We do not collect usage analytics, diagnostics, or device identifiers.
- We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
- We do not track users across apps or websites.

## Children's privacy

- The app is designed for children and does not collect personal information.
- The app can be used without creating an account or providing any personal details.

## Permissions

- The app does not request access to contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location, or other sensitive device data.

## Changes to this policy

If our practices change, we will update this policy and the App Store listing accordingly.

If you have questions about this policy, please reach out via the App Store support contact on our listing.
